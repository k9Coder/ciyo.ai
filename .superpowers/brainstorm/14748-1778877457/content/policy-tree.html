What each level means

Policy

The tenant's entire ruleset. One active policy at a time. Versioned — can rollback.

Division

Org scope. Rules inside only apply to users in this division. "Global" = applies to everyone.

Subject

A named concern — a case, a project, a data category. Groups related rules together. This is what replaces "matter".

Rule

The actual detection logic: what to look for (keywords, patterns, entropy) + what to do (warn/block) + where (which sites).
