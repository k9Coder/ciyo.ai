**OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications — the baseline your AI policy should map to**
https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/
OWASP's canonical list of the ten most critical risks in LLM-powered apps, including sensitive information disclosure and insecure output handling.
Why it matters for DLP: LLM06 (Sensitive Information Disclosure) is the DLP problem, formalized — data leaves via prompts, then potentially resurfaces in outputs or training. If your AI acceptable-use policy doesn't map controls to these categories, auditors and vendors increasingly will. Useful shared vocabulary for security and eng teams.
