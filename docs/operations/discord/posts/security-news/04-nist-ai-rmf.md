**NIST AI Risk Management Framework — the vendor-neutral scaffolding for AI governance**
https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
NIST's voluntary framework (AI RMF 1.0, plus its Generative AI Profile) for identifying, measuring, and managing AI risk across the lifecycle: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.
Why it matters for DLP: When leadership asks "what's our AI risk posture," this is the structure to answer in — and data leakage through employee AI use sits squarely in the Map and Manage functions. It's also becoming the reference point in procurement questionnaires and board reporting, so anchoring your AI usage controls to it saves you the argument later.
