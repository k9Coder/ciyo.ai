**Community guidelines**

Welcome. This community exists so people running DLP don't have to learn every lesson the expensive way. A few rules keep it that way:

1. **Be a professional.** No harassment, no spam, no drive-by self-promotion. If you want to share something you built or sell, ask a mod first.
2. **Practice what we preach.** Never post real secrets, credentials, customer names, or internal data — here of all places. Sanitize every screenshot, config, and log line before it leaves your clipboard. If you accidentally post something sensitive, delete it and DM a mod immediately.
3. **Specifics welcome, attacks aren't.** Security conversations get better with detail — real scenarios, real configs, real failure stories. But keep it legal and defensive: no requests for help attacking systems you don't own or operate.
4. **Right room, faster answer.** Product and setup questions → #help-and-questions. Template ideas → #template-requests. Policy submissions go through the form in #share-your-policy — we don't accept direct posts there.
5. **Moderation.** Mods may remove content that breaks these rules; repeat or serious violations mean removal from the server. Questions about a moderation call → DM anyone with the `mykka.ai Team` role.
