**HR pasted salary bands into an AI tool to "make the table look nicer"**

An HR ops manager at a 600-person software company found this in her first mykka review: someone pasted the entire comp band spreadsheet — names, titles, salaries, equity — into an AI tool to reformat it into a cleaner table. Formatting help. That's it. That's how the most radioactive file in the company almost left the building.

Their policy:

- **Data type:** employee data — fuzzy dictionary built from the HRIS export (names + employee IDs), pattern rules for salary figures paired with names
- **Destination:** all AI tools
- **Action:** block for the HR data set, log everything else for quarterly review

I keep saying this: nobody in these stories was trying to leak anything. Every one of them was trying to do their job faster. Write policies for that person.
