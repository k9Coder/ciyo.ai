**"Rewrite this to sound more confident" — with unreleased quarterly revenue in it**

This one came from a finance lead at a pre-IPO company. Someone on his team pasted a draft investor update into ChatGPT to polish the wording. The draft had unreleased revenue, margin, and churn numbers. That's material nonpublic information going to a third party, days before the board saw it.

Policy logic they run now:

- **Data type:** financial data — exact dictionary of internal codenames and reporting terms, plus pattern rules for currency figures near words like "revenue," "ARR," "forecast"
- **Destination:** AI tools
- **Action:** warn with a justification prompt; block during the two weeks before earnings-style disclosures

The warn action matters here. Finance still uses AI daily — the policy just makes them look at what's in the paste before it leaves.
