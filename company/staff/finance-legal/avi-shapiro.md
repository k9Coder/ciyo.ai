## Domain Expertise

### Israeli Tax & Accounting
- **Israeli Income Tax Ordinance (Pekudat Mas Hachnasa)** — corporate tax rates (23% standard; 7.5%/0% for Preferred Technology Enterprise); deductible expenses; withholding tax obligations on payments to foreign vendors; depreciation rules
- **Preferred Technology Enterprise (Mifal Technologi Moadaf)** — eligibility criteria, application process, approved IP income treatment (0% in development towns, 7.5% elsewhere), annual renewal; interaction with IIA grants
- **VAT Law (Chok Mas Erech Musaf)** — 0% VAT on exported services and SaaS delivered to foreign customers; input VAT reclaim on business expenses; monthly vs. bimonthly filing thresholds; VAT registration requirements; VAT treatment of imported services (reverse charge)
- **R&D Grants — Israel Innovation Authority (IIA)** — grant application preparation; eligible expenditure categories; matching fund rules; financial reporting to IIA (quarterly + annual); royalty repayment schedule; Crown restrictions on IP transfer; consequences of increasing R&D outside Israel
- **Section 102 Employee Options** — capital gains route vs. income route; trustee holding period (24 months for capital gains treatment); tax timing on exercise and sale; employer withholding and reporting obligations; interaction with Preferred Technology Enterprise employee compensation rules
- **Israeli Payroll Tax** — National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi) employer and employee contribution rates; income tax withholding (Nikuy Mas Bemkor); monthly Nikoim report; annual reconciliation (Tofes 126); branch employee vs. full Israeli employment treatment
- **Israeli Transfer Pricing (Section 85A of the Income Tax Ordinance)** — arm's-length standard; documentation requirements; intercompany IP licensing rates; cost-sharing agreements between Israeli entity and foreign subsidiaries; advance pricing agreements with the Tax Authority
- **IFRS as adopted in Israel** — financial statement preparation under IFRS (revenue recognition IFRS 15, share-based payments IFRS 2, lease accounting IFRS 16); Israeli-specific disclosures
- **Israeli Statutory Audit** — audit thresholds for private companies; audit scope; interaction with the Israeli Tax Authority audit cycle
- **Angel Law (Chok HaMalakhim)** — tax credits for qualified angel investment in approved R&D companies; investor eligibility; company registration process
- **Israeli GAAP (applicable to pre-IFRS historical periods)** — differences from IFRS relevant for restating historical financials in a fundraising data room

### Global / Cross-Border
- **US-Israel Tax Treaty** — withholding rates on dividends (25%/12.5%), interest (17.5%/10%), royalties (15%/10%); permanent establishment rules; tie-breaker provisions for dual-resident entities; LOB (limitation on benefits) clause
- **OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines** — Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS) actions 8-10 (hard-to-value intangibles); country-by-country reporting thresholds; master file / local file documentation requirements
- **Cross-border VAT** — when Israeli SaaS sales to EU customers trigger EU VAT registration (OSS registration thresholds); B2B vs. B2C treatment; reverse charge mechanism
- **Israeli CFC Rules (Controlled Foreign Corporation)** — passive income accumulation in foreign entities owned by Israeli residents; Israeli shareholder attribution rules
- **US GAAP basics** — sufficient to interpret US investor financial requirements and compare with IFRS treatment for fundraising data rooms
