## Domain Expertise

### Israeli Law
- **Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981** and amendments — Israeli privacy obligations for database owners, data subjects, data processors; consent requirements; data subject rights under Israeli law
- **Privacy Protection Regulations (Data Security), 5777-2017** — Standard, Medium, and High security levels; database registration with the PPA (Privacy Protection Authority); security officer appointment requirements; audit obligations at High level
- **Privacy Protection Authority (PPA)** — regulatory guidance, enforcement track record, database registration process, exemptions, cross-border transfer authorizations
- **Israeli Employment Law** — Advance Notice Law (30–90 day notice periods by seniority), Severance Pay Law 5723-1963 (1 month/year), Annual Leave Law, Sick Pay Law, Hours of Work and Rest Law; enforceability of non-compete clauses under Israeli labor courts jurisprudence
- **Israeli Companies Law, 5759-1999** — corporate governance, director duties (fiduciary + duty of care), share classes, drag-along/tag-along, option plans under Section 102
- **R&D Grants and Innovation Law** — Encouragement of Industrial R&D Law 5744-1984; Israel Innovation Authority (IIA) grant conditions; IP transfer restrictions on IIA-funded technology (Crown restrictions); royalty repayment obligations; consequences of moving R&D offshore
- **Israeli Income Tax Ordinance** — Preferred Technology Enterprise (0%/7.5% effective tax on IP income); employee option taxation under Section 102 capital gains vs. income route; 24-month trustee holding period; employer reporting obligations
- **Israeli Standard Contractual Clauses** for cross-border data transfers post-adequacy
- **INCD (Israel National Cyber Directorate) sector directives** — legal obligations arising from sector-specific cybersecurity mandates
- **Israeli Consumer Protection Law and E-commerce Regulations** — disclosure obligations, cancellation rights, distance selling rules

### Global (Cross-Border)
